# Frozen active sparse-observation experiment

Original RealPDE, seed42, frozen four-step DiPred/XPF marginal heads. Train819 windows/46 trajectories; calibration90/10; development108/12, late120 and future72 on the same12 development trajectories. These are exploratory reused trajectories, not independent confirmation.

Task boundary: history frames[s,s+20) are fully observed. Issue P for[s+20,s+40) at time s+20 and select locations using only X/P/F and training artifacts. At s+25 receive the two velocity components at the fifth forecast-frame sample (array index4, timestamp convention: available after five forecast frames). Immediately reissue predictions for the remaining15 samples, target array indices5:20. Primary metric is physical u/v MSE over those15 frames only. This is an additional-observation update task, not an improvement to the original no-new-observation20→20 forecast. Do not fill absent observations with zeros or feed incomplete histories into DiPred.

Candidate sites are an8×16 grid at y=2+4j,x=2+4k on the32×64 field. One action costs one site/two scalar measurements of the same fifth frame. Budgets0,1,2,4,8,16,32;8 is the primary fixed budget. All locations for a budget are committed before reading any newly observed value. Primary data use the recorded PIV values without added sensor noise; unresolved residual modes in the assimilation model are model error, not a claim about sensor noise.

Common assimilator: low-rank linear least-squares conditional update learned from actual training forecast residuals. At each issue, scale each residual coordinate by the standard deviation of the fixed32 midpoint marginal nodes. Floor scales at5% of training component residual RMS. Fit a trajectory-balanced *uncentered second-moment* SVD of these normalized signed residuals, retaining at most32 factors. Unmodeled diagonal remainder is clipped at.01 in normalized units. Use ranks8/16/32, remainder multipliers.25/1/4 and output gains.5/1 as a small calibration-only grid, selected by mean relative MSE under the common uniform policy at budgets4/8/16/32. No observation-selection policy is used to tune this assimilator. Zero observations reproduce raw P exactly, with no fitted intercept added. The second moment gives the best linear through-origin update under the fitted surrogate; it is not asserted to be the true conditional Gaussian covariance.

For current physical factor matrix A and observation rows H, latent posterior surrogate covariance is S=(I+H'R^-1H)^-1. The issued correction is gain×A S H'R^-1(observed−P_observed). The explicit observation interface accepts only selected indices and their2-component values; no unselected truth enters the update. All policies share this same operator and hyperparameters.

Policies: spatially stratified uniform grid; nested geometric farthest-point space filling; random selection (one executable draw plus a16-draw expected-random diagnostic from one fixed RNG stream, no retraining seeds); maximum marginal variance; maximum marginal predicted squared error (variance plus marginal mean bias squared); greedy predicted downstream MSE reduction (VOI); training-average-scale VOI selection as a conditioning ablation. Greedy VOI uses the trace decrease of the remaining-field error second moment, accounts for redundancy with earlier chosen sites, and requires no observation values. It is a standard A-optimal/linear-Gaussian-style criterion, not a novelty claim or a strict physical information guarantee. Primary-source context: https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.6323 and https://arxiv.org/abs/1308.4084 .
