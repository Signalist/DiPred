# Frozen second mechanism: observable future-error modes

Motivation after retaining the first model's complete results: its8-site VOI did not beat variance/risk selection, and predicted reductions substantially exceeded realized reductions. The first SVD prioritized total normalized error energy; it did not directly prioritize future errors recoverable from the lead5 sensor plane. This is an exploratory follow-up on reused splits, not a fresh confirmation or a retroactively preplanned first model.

Keep the exact original observation/issuance/budget/metric protocol, the same cached actual forecasts and marginal scales, all policies, and the same common-update fairness rule. Fit a second small linear mechanism from training only: O is the256 standardized lead5 sensor residuals, T is the standardized remaining15-frame residual. Use trajectory-balanced second moments Coo and Cto. For shrinkage lambda in.1/.5/.9, define Roo=(1−lambda)Coo+lambda diag(Coo), and cross=(1−lambda)Cto. Whiten cross with the Cholesky factor of Roo; its top8/16/32 singular directions yield the observable future-error factors B and latent-to-observation cross moment H. Retain the full256×256 observation second moment, including correlated unresolved errors. This remains a rank≤32 linear predictor, not a new neural backbone.

For a selected sensor subset S, update with gain×B H_S Roo_SS^-1 residual_S, with physical scales restored. Gains.5/1 and the3 ranks/3 shrinkages are selected exclusively by the same uniform calibration budgets4/8/16/32 criterion. Current held-out Y does not select models or locations. VOI uses the same predicted downstream MSE reduction and now conditions on the full observation second moment, accounting for redundant measurements beyond the retained factors. Greedy selection is still committed before values arrive.

Preserve first-family results under the parent folder. Put second-family weights, selection, all results, uncertainty, audits and latency in predictive/. A positive result must survive comparisons against risk/variance and expected-random at equal site budget. Fewer-site claims require actual held-out budget-curve comparison, not the surrogate's predicted decrease. If the second family also fails, interpret the two mechanisms' bounded evidence without asserting that every possible VOI strategy is invalid.
