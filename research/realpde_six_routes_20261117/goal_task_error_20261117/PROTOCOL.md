# Goal-oriented task error pilot (frozen before fitting/evaluation)

Scope: original RealPDE released real measurements, seed42, same train819/46 trajectories and calibration90/10; development108/12 and later120/72 windows are reused exploratory trajectories, not independent confirmation. No hidden test or simulation data. Original observed20→20 setting, no added observations or online feedback. Frozen backbone and point forecast stay unchanged; the output improved here is a scalar task estimate, not an altered field or free rollout.

Four fixed discrete tasks on the 32×64 measured grid over all20 future frames: (1) mean planar kinetic energy .5 mean(u²+v²); (2) mean temporal fluctuation energy .5 mean((u−mean_time u)²+(v−mean_time v)²); (3) mean enstrophy .5 mean(curl²), centered spatial stencil on interior30×62; (4) mean streamwise velocity in the rightmost quarter of the measured grid. The fourth is a regional transport proxy, not a surface force or whole-domain conservation law. Spatial derivatives use actual signed HDF5 coordinate increments; nominal dt is the median HDF5 t increment. SI unit labels are withheld because inspected files/documentation have no explicit unit metadata. No substitution of NACA0025 benchmark details for this NACA4418 release.

Primary metric: trajectory-balanced mean of four squared task prediction errors divided by each task's training baseline squared-error mean. Also report each task, raw MAE, signed bias, calibration, development/late/future, per-trajectory and Re. Fits/normalizations/PCA use train only. One global ridge and shrink per method chosen only by the calibration primary score. Ridge .01/.1/1/10, shrink0/.25/.5/1. No changing the primary task after results.

Controls and mechanisms:
- Unchanged J(P), four training mean signed-error constants.
- Direct signed task-error ridge on16 training-only PCs from P, X+P, F_last, same-four-call F_det4_1500 and6000, X+P+F_last. Same68 trainable coefficients each; PCA is counted separately. XPF32 control has132 coefficients.
- Observable kinematic descriptors, with16 PCs: task statistics for X/P plus divergence and material-acceleration summaries. The latter are incomplete kinematic mismatches, NOT a closed Navier–Stokes residual or quantities assumed zero. A separate task-weighted descriptor uses exact task gradients applied to three available perturbation fields (−divergence-gradient, −material acceleration, history persistence−P). Both norm-only and task-weighted controls fit the same readout family.
- Exact task Taylor decomposition: delta J = L(P,R)+Q(R) for the three quadratic tasks, and L only for regional velocity. Fit seven outputs with16 XPF PCs (119 coefficients); clamp predicted Q to >=0. Report dropping Q as a frozen diagnostic, not a new selection. Without clipping and using the same linear fit, summing L/Q is algebraically the direct model; this is not claimed as novel physics.
- Fixed16 physical residual POD modes and XPF16 coefficient ridge (272 coefficients), evaluated through exact J(P+Rhat). Add a separately reported training constant unresolved Q(R−R_projection) (three coefficients) to diagnose missing residual energy. Truth-projected POD only appears as an oracle.

All main models selected before dev evaluation. No claiming diffusion-specific F benefit without deterministic/capacity controls. This bounded pilot aims for useful signed task error prediction and diagnosis, not a new theorem.

Certificates: exact finite-dimensional Taylor identities and Cauchy–Schwarz inequalities can be audited. Their residual norms are not available at issue time. A secondary diagnostic replaces each norm with the maximum over calibration trajectory maxima and measures empirical violations, alongside a task-specific absolute-error envelope. Ten calibration trajectories and regime reuse do NOT establish a conditional, OOD or closed-PDE guarantee. No increasing quantile counts; empirical envelopes are a boundary check, not the main research objective.

Implementation audits: independent NumPy task/Taylor calculations; centered stencils on an analytic linear field; exact oracle inequality; actual batch1 public X-only inference for selected models; label/future intervention; fit/eval trajectory separation; source/protocol hashes; interleaved batch1 latency after initialization. Retain all negative results and source fixes.
